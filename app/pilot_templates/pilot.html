JSON field-by-field guide
purchase_order
One purchase order: po_id (PO number), vendor_id (supplier ID), vendor_name (supplier name), currency (USD), status (OPEN or CLOSED).
purchase_order.lines
Items ordered: line_number (row number), sku (unique item code), description (item name), quantity (units ordered), unit_price (price per unit), gl_account (nonblank ledger account).
receipts
List of receiving records: receipt_id (unique receiving record ID), po_id (the same PO number).
receipts[].lines
Items received: sku (item code), quantity_received (units that arrived). Use distinct SKUs within each receipt.
Numbers and limits
Use quoted nonnegative decimals for quantities and prices (up to 10 digits and 4 decimal places). Use a number for line_number. Supply 1–100 PO lines and 1–30 receipts with 1–100 lines each.
