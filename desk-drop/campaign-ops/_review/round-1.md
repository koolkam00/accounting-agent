## A1. Detail behind R1-02 (office declines at the door)

What went wrong in the exception replay, step by step:

1. **The report never generates.** The report generator refuses for want of a day-5 count at the office that declined, and no sheet check explains why.
2. **Gate 2 and the exports go wrong.**
   - The campaign's `units_depleted_day5` stays blank for good, and H reads the blank as 0.
   - `survey_responses_min_office` becomes 0, so `gate2_responses` reads "miss" and the office counts against Gate 2.
   - H's `offices` reads 10 for 9 offices delivered.
3. **The office is asked about a product it refused.**
   - Day-3 text: how many are left, plus a nudge for survey answers.
   - Day-5 email: "Thank you for making room on the shelf".
   - Office debrief: "How did it land with your team?" and "handed to pantry owner".
4. **The brand emails claim a report that does not exist.**
   - The T+7 email to the brand says "Your one-page report is attached", though no PDF exists.
   - The brand debrief lists numbers "from the report".
5. **Nothing tells the founder what to do.** The dashboard shows 0 checks.
6. **The replay harness misses it.** `dryrun.py` counts only formula errors and disagreements, so it reports "0 problems".
