**The company in this file.** The company's legal name is set once, in the `[COMPANY NAME: …]` slot in the agreement's first line. Replace it with the exact name on the filed Articles of Organization before sending.

**What it is for.** The agreement a brand signs to buy one Office Placement Campaign. Part 1 is a fill-in sheet with this campaign's numbers and dates. Part 2 is the fixed terms, the same for every brand.

**When it is used.** On a brand's yes (plan, Phase 2 "Close and collect", step 3), sent with the invoice. Weeks 4 to 7 (2026-11-16 to 2026-12-11, derived, founder to confirm). For campaign zero, the canonical drop is Tuesday 2027-01-12 with ship-by Tuesday 2026-12-22 (CONTRACTS section 3).

**Sign only after the company is formed.** Unlike the office page, this one moves money and names the company as an additional insured on the brand's policy. The plan forms the company at the first verbal brand yes, before you invoice. So: file the Articles, get the EIN, open the bank account, then send this agreement and the invoice together. Section 13 is a fallback in case a brand insists on signing first; never send an invoice before the company and its bank account exist. Send it only after the attorney hour (file 08) as well: this agreement is what most of that hour is for, and the plan puts the hour before the first invoice.

## Part 2 · Terms

**1. What we do.** We place your product in each office, or have the office place it, within one business day of delivery, in the pantry the office names and at eye level where the pantry allows (we ask each office to place it there), with a display card showing your two sentences, the allergen statement copied word for word from your label (including any "may contain" line), and a QR code unique to that office and campaign. We send you the card text before printing. No other brand in your category (Part 1) is in the same drop. For each office we send you a placement photo from the day it was placed (taken by us or by the office) and a later photo from day 3 or day 5, and top up from your reserve where an office runs out. We run a three-question anonymous employee survey. We send you a one-page report by T+7 and hold a 20-minute debrief call by T+10. "T" is the drop day; T−n and T+n count business days (Monday to Friday, not US federal holidays). No drop day or window falls on 18 to 31 December, 18 to 31 August, or the Monday-to-Friday week containing 4 July or its observed holiday (the "blackouts").

**2. What you do.** (a) Ship the units and reserve in Part 1, freight prepaid, to arrive by T−5; ship by the date in Part 1, and no later than T−10. (b) Every unit is individually packaged, sealed, shelf-stable, needs no refrigeration, and has at least 60 days of shelf life left on the drop day. It contains no alcohol, cannabis, CBD or THC, is not a dietary supplement (no Supplement Facts panel), and neither its label nor the two sentences you give us make a health claim, including structure or function wording such as "supports gut health". We may edit or decline card sentences that do. (c) With or before the shipment, send us: an ingredient and allergen sheet; your logo files and two sentences for the card; the discount code or landing page; and a certificate of insurance showing product liability coverage of at least \$1,000,000 per occurrence, with the Company added as an additional insured. Keep that coverage and our additional-insured status in force from your ship date until 12 months after the window closes, or until the latest best-by date on the units if that is later. If your policy is claims-made, its retroactive date must be on or before your ship date, and you will keep the coverage in force or buy an extended reporting period for that time. Product does not ship until the certificate is in hand. (d) Tell us at once about any recall or safety issue with the product, from signing until 12 months after the window closes.

**3. Price and payment.** The price and payment terms are in Part 1. We invoice for payment by bank transfer. Payment due on signing or before shipping must reach us before we confirm your ship date; if it has not reached us within five business days of our invoice, we may cancel the campaign and refund anything you paid. The second standard payment is due on our placement confirmation: our email, sent by T+1, listing the offices where your product was placed, with each one's placement photo. We refund or credit \$150 for any office where it was not placed by T+1 (section 8). An office placed by T+1 whose photo reaches us later is added by a short follow-up email and is not refunded. We invoice that day and may hold the report until payment arrives. Prices do not include sales tax; if tax applies, it is shown on the invoice. **Founding tier only:** your next two campaigns with us are priced at \$120 per office for up to 10 offices (any office beyond 10 at \$150), paid in full before you ship. If we deliver everything in section 1, you will, within 30 days after the debrief, give us a short written statement of your honest experience, and allow us to publish it with your brand name together with a case study drawn from your report. When we publish them, we will say you were a founding brand at a founding price.

**4. No guarantee of results.** We do not guarantee sales, purchases, scans, survey responses or any other result. The report states what we observed, kept separate: units delivered, placed and depleted; survey responses; QR scans; and code redemptions you report to us. Incremental sales are not measured, and the report says so.

**5. Your promises about the product.** You promise that the product is safe; that its label complies with federal and state labeling law, including the declaration of the nine major food allergens; that it is within its shelf life; and that you have the right to let us use the logo and words you give us. We keep every unit in its original sealed retail package: we never open, alter, relabel or repack a unit, and we put nothing on it. We may move sealed units between shipping cartons to make up each office's count. We store sealed cases at room temperature, clean, dry, off the floor, away from heat and as the label directs, and we record best-by dates and lot numbers on arrival. We do not place a unit that arrives damaged or with less than 60 days of shelf life left on the drop day; we photograph it, send you the counts, and return it at your cost or dispose of it, as you tell us. If anyone reports a problem with the product, we remove it from that office and tell you the same day. If you ask us to pull the product (for example, for a recall), we remove it from every office within one business day.

**6. Product claims.** You will defend us against any claim by anyone else arising from the product, and pay any resulting settlement, judgment and reasonable lawyers' fees, except to the extent the claim is caused by our mishandling of the product. We will tell you promptly about any claim and help you respond to it. You control the defense, but you may not settle a claim in a way that admits fault for us or requires anything of us without our written consent.

**7. Limit on our liability.** Our total liability to you for anything to do with this agreement is limited to the fees you paid us for this campaign.

**8. Cancellation and slips.** You may cancel by email at any time before the drop day (T). If you cancel before T−10 (the date in Part 1), we refund everything you paid, less \$100 for setup work already done. If a drop moves more than once for these reasons, or by more than three weeks in all, you may cancel by email for a full refund. If we cancel, we refund everything you paid. If an office leaves, declines your product, or has not placed it by T+1, and we cannot replace it with another roster office, we refund that office's share (\$120 founding, \$150 standard) or take it off an unpaid invoice.
