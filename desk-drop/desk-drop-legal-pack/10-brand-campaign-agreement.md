# 10 · Brand campaign agreement: notes for the founder

Version 0.5 · 2026-10-24

**The company in this file.** The company's legal name is set once, in the `[COMPANY NAME: …]` slot in the agreement's first line. Replace it with the exact name on the filed Articles of Organization before sending.

**What it is for.** The agreement a brand signs to buy one Office Placement Campaign. Part 1 is a fill-in sheet with this campaign's numbers and dates. Part 2 is the fixed terms, the same for every brand.

**When it is used.** On a brand's yes (plan, Phase 2 "Close and collect", step 3), sent with the invoice. Weeks 4 to 7 (2026-11-16 to 2026-12-11, derived, founder to confirm). For campaign zero, the canonical drop is Tuesday 2027-01-12 with ship-by Tuesday 2026-12-22 (CONTRACTS section 3).

**Sign only after the company is formed.** Unlike the office page, this one moves money and names the company as an additional insured on the brand's policy. The plan forms the company at the first verbal brand yes, before you invoice. So: file the Articles, get the EIN, open the bank account, then send this agreement and the invoice together. Section 13 is a fallback in case a brand insists on signing first; never send an invoice before the company and its bank account exist. Send it only after the attorney hour (file 08) as well: this agreement is what most of that hour is for, and the plan puts the hour before the first invoice.

**How to fill in Part 1.** Most blanks come from, or go back to, the paid-brands row (CONTRACTS 6.4), which carries every brand-intake column: `brand_name`, `contact_name`, `contact_title`, `contact_email`, `contact_phone`, `product_sku`, `category`, `tier`, `price_usd`, `offices_count`, `payment_terms`, `unused_product_election`, `discount_code`, `landing_url`, `retailer_line`. The dates have no column there; work them out from the drop day. Use the business-day rule in CONTRACTS section 3 (Monday to Friday, no US federal holidays). Check that no day of the window falls in a blackout.

**How to send it.** PDF through the e-signature tool (rendered copy: `rendered/10-brand-campaign-agreement.pdf`). In the same email, send the certificate request (file 11) and ask for the allergen sheet, logo files, two card sentences and the discount code or landing page (plan, "Close and collect", step 4). Record `agreement_signed_date`. Product ships only when (a) payment due before shipping has landed and (b) the brand's certificate checks out (file 11 checklist).

## Part 2 · Terms

**1. What we do.** We place your product in each office, or have the office place it, within one business day of delivery, at eye level in the pantry the office names, with a display card showing your two sentences, the allergen statement copied word for word from your label (including any "may contain" line), and a QR code unique to that office and campaign. We send you the card text before printing. No other brand in your category (Part 1) is in the same drop. For each office we take a placement photo on the drop day and a later photo on day 3 or day 5, and top up from your reserve where an office runs out. We run a three-question anonymous employee survey. We send you a one-page report by T+7 and hold a 20-minute debrief call by T+10. "T" is the drop day; T−n and T+n count business days (Monday to Friday, not US federal holidays). No drop day or window falls on 18 to 31 December, 18 to 31 August, or the Monday-to-Friday week containing 4 July or its observed holiday (the "blackouts").

**2. What you do.** (a) Ship the units and reserve in Part 1, freight prepaid, to arrive by T−5; ship by T−10. (b) Every unit is individually packaged, sealed, shelf-stable, needs no refrigeration, and has at least 60 days of shelf life left on arrival. (c) With or before the shipment, send us: an ingredient and allergen sheet; your logo files and two sentences for the card; the discount code or landing page; and a certificate of insurance showing product liability coverage of at least \$1,000,000 per occurrence, with the Company added as an additional insured, kept in force through the end of the window. Product does not ship until the certificate is in hand. (d) Tell us before the drop about any recall or safety issue with the product.

**3. Price and payment.** The price and payment terms are in Part 1. We invoice for payment by bank transfer. Payment due before shipping (the founding price, or the first standard payment) must reach us before we confirm your ship date. The second standard payment is due within seven days of our placement confirmation, which is the email sending you every office's placement photo; we may hold the report until it arrives. Prices do not include sales tax; if tax applies, it is shown on the invoice. **Founding tier only:** your next two campaigns with us are priced at \$120 per office, paid in full before you ship. If we deliver everything in section 1, you will, within 30 days after the debrief, give us a short written statement of your honest experience, and allow us to publish it with your brand name together with a case study drawn from your report. When we publish them, we will say you were a founding brand at a founding price.

**4. No guarantee of results.** We do not guarantee sales, purchases, scans, survey responses or any other result. The report states what we observed, kept separate: units delivered, placed and depleted; survey responses; QR scans; and code redemptions you report to us. Incremental sales are not measured, and the report says so.

**5. Your promises about the product.** You promise that the product is safe; that its label complies with federal and state labeling law, including the declaration of the nine major food allergens; that it is within its shelf life; and that you have the right to let us use the logo and words you give us. We keep every unit in its original sealed retail package: we never open, alter, relabel or repack a unit, and we put nothing on it. We may move sealed units between shipping cartons to make up each office's count. We store sealed cases at room temperature, clean, dry, off the floor, away from heat and as the label directs, and we record best-by dates and lot numbers on arrival. We do not place a unit that arrives damaged or with less than 60 days of shelf life; we photograph it, send you the counts, and return it at your cost or dispose of it, as you tell us. If anyone reports a problem with the product, we remove it from that office and tell you the same day. If you ask us to pull the product (for example, for a recall), we remove it from every office within one business day.

**6. Product claims.** You will defend us against any claim by anyone else arising from the product, and pay any resulting settlement, judgment and reasonable lawyers' fees, except to the extent the claim is caused by our mishandling of the product. We will tell you promptly about any claim and help you respond to it. You control the defense, but you may not settle a claim in a way that admits fault for us or requires anything of us without our written consent.

**7. Limit on our liability.** Our total liability to you for anything to do with this agreement is limited to the fees you paid us for this campaign.

**8. Cancellation and slips.** You may cancel by email. If you cancel before T−10, we refund everything you paid, less \$100 for setup work already done. If you cancel on or after T−10, we keep 50% of the campaign price, because by then we have held the drop day with every office, prepared the cards and kept your category closed to other brands for that drop; we refund anything you paid above that, and you owe nothing more. If your product has not arrived by T−3, we move the drop one week at no charge and tell every office the same day; if the new date falls in a blackout, it moves to the next Tuesday or Wednesday after it. If the product misses the new date's T−3 too, either of us may treat the campaign as cancelled by you on or after T−10. If weather, illness, a building closure or anything else outside our control stops a drop, we move it to the next open Tuesday or Wednesday and tell you the same day. If we cancel, we refund everything you paid. If an office leaves or declines your product and we cannot replace it with another roster office before the drop, we refund that office's share (\$120 founding, \$150 standard).

**9. Unused product.** At the end of the window, leftover and unused reserve units are returned to you at your cost or released to the offices, as you chose in Part 1. If you chose return and have not sent a prepaid return label within ten business days after the window closes, we release them to the offices. A unit that has been placed in one office is never moved to another.

**10. Photos, results and your report.** We may use placement photos, which never show identifiable people, and survey results that identify no person or office, in our own materials; we do not identify an office there unless it agreed to be named. You may use the report for your own business, but do not publish an office's name, or a photo that identifies an office, unless we confirm that office agreed. You let us use your name, logo and card sentences on the display card and in the report.

**11. Our offices stay ours to introduce.** For six months after the window closes, you will not approach any office we named to you for this campaign to arrange placements or sampling with it directly. This does not limit ordinary retail or online sales, general advertising, or replying to an office that contacts you on its own.

**12. Confidentiality and employee data.** Before you sign, we describe offices only by neighborhood, industry and Tuesday headcount, and name only offices that agreed to be named. After you sign, we share office names and addresses only as needed to run this campaign. Until two years after the window closes, you will keep them confidential and use them only for this campaign. We keep any sales or redemption numbers you share confidential, except in your report and, for a founding brand, the case study. We collect no personal data about office employees and will not ask offices for any. The only exception: if you chose the newsletter option in Part 1, an employee may tick an unticked box to share an email address with you; we pass those addresses to you once, as a list separate from survey answers, and from then on your privacy policy governs them.

**13. If the Company is not yet formed.** If the Company has not been formed when this is signed, [FOUNDER NAME] signs for it in advance. Once formed, the Company takes over this agreement in [FOUNDER NAME]'s place, and you agree to that now. We will not invoice you until the Company is formed.
