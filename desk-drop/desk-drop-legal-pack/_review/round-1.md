### 2.2 Broker: would a carrier issue the additional-insured wording as written?

### 2.3 Food-safety inspector: does the scope match a sealed, shelf-stable, no-repack operation?

**Pass.** `07`'s question, facts 1 to 9, `06` section 1, `10` section 5 and `11`'s handling paragraph describe the same operation. Sealed retail units are never opened, changed or relabeled. Cases are held at room temperature for two to four weeks. Arrival checks are done, and damaged or short-dated units are set aside. Sealed units move from shipping cartons into office boxes. The pack discloses that move and does not hide it: follow-up 3 asks whether it counts as repacking. The statute quotes match the sources I opened (AGM §500-a(2) and (4), §500-b(3) \$400; agriculture.ny.gov: "This license applies to any food warehouse facility within New York in which food is held for commercial distribution", Metro New York and Long Island Office (718) 722-2876, 55 Hanson Place). The only problems are citation pinpoints (R1-14).

### 2.4 Pantry owner: could they sign the office agreement in five minutes without calling anyone?

**Borderline.** The page is one Letter page and 831 words (pdftotext): about four minutes of reading. The words are plain, the "In short" line comes first, there is nothing to pay, and leaving takes a week's notice. Two things are likely to make a pantry owner pause or forward it: the counterparty label collision (**R1-05**) and the "Draft for attorney review" banner at the top of the page they are asked to sign (**R1-07**). Fix both and it passes.
