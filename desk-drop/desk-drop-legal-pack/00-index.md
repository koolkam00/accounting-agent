# 00 · Index: what each document is for and when you use it

Version 0.8 · 2026-10-24

**What this pack is.** The drafts and checklists that let you form the company, insure it, sign offices and brands, and stay compliant, in the order the plan uses them. Every file opens with the review heading above and stays a draft until an attorney clears it. Nothing has been filed, sent, signed, bound, applied for or paid.

**Name gate: HOLD.** The name check (file 01) found a business already trading as "DeskDrop" and a USPTO refusal of DESK-DROP as descriptive. No print run and no public launch under the working name until you decide (file 01, "Decision needed"). Private discovery calls can go ahead.

**Dates.** Every date here is derived from the canonical calendar (CONTRACTS section 3: week 1 starts Monday 2026-10-26; campaign zero drops Tuesday 2027-01-12) and is "derived, founder to confirm".

## Not in this pack, and when it comes

- Trademark application: after campaign zero, if it was a pass (plan, setup checklist "After campaign zero"; the legal table says after campaign one succeeds), so from about Mon 2027-01-25, and only after the attorney advises on the name (file 08, item 1). Fee in `figures.csv`.
- FDA food facility registration: only if cases are stored somewhere other than your home, and then before the first case arrives; no fee (file 07, follow-up 7).
- Food warehouse license application: only if the state says one is needed (file 07, "What to do with the answer").
- Sales tax registration: only if the accountant says any part of the fee is taxable (file 09, question 3).
- Workers' compensation: the day anyone is hired, including part-time delivery help (plan; revisit at Phase 4).
