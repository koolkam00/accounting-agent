# 11 · Office participation agreement: notes for the founder

Version 0.2 · 2026-10-24

**What it is for.** The one page a founding office signs. It records what both sides agreed so nobody has to remember it. It is not a contract with penalties: the office never owes money or damages, and either side can leave on seven days' notice.

**When it is used.** At step 4 of the plan's Phase 1 path from first call to signature: the pantry owner has said yes, and you send this with the intake form. Weeks 3 to 5 of the plan (2026-11-09 to 2026-11-27, derived, founder to confirm). This is likely before the company is formed; section 9 of the agreement handles that.

**How to fill it in.** Everything above the numbered sections comes from the office intake form (columns in CONTRACTS 6.3: `company`, `office_address`, `floor_suite`, `pantry_owner_name`, `pantry_owner_title`, `contact_phone` and `contact_email` (ask for the pantry owner's own if `pantry_owner_is_contact = N`), `receiving_type`, `receiving_window`, `coi_required`). Fill those blanks before sending so the pantry owner only checks, ticks the naming boxes and signs. The tick boxes in section 6 map to `founding_roster_named` (Y if the first box is ticked).

**How to send it.** As a PDF through the free e-signature tool the operations workstream sets up (rendered copy: `rendered/11-office-participation-agreement.pdf`). Put a signature field and a date field for each side, and tick-box fields for section 6. Sign second, the same day. Save the signed PDF and record `agreement_signed_date` and `agreement_file` in the signed-offices row.

**Signing before the company exists (why section 9 reads as it does).** Offices sign in weeks 3 to 5; the plan forms the company at the first verbal brand yes. So most office agreements will be signed first. Three ways to handle it were weighed:
1. Wait to sign until the company is formed. Rejected: it stalls the scarce side for days, for no benefit to the office.
2. Sign personally now, then ask every office to sign a transfer later. Rejected: five extra signatures and five chances for "let me check with someone."
3. **Chosen:** you sign for the company before it is formed, and the office agrees in advance that the company takes your place once formed. The company then adopts these agreements in its first written consent (file 30, Schedule B), and you send each office one email (below). Nothing changes for the office. Attorney to confirm this releases you personally under New York law.

Email to send each office once the company is formed (it is a relationship message; no footer needed):

> Subject: Our participation agreement: a one-line update
> Hi [Name], a quick formality: the company's New York registration is now complete, so as section 9 of our agreement says, it has taken over our agreement from me. Nothing else changes. [FOUNDER NAME]

**What not to change without the attorney.** Section 7 (no money, no penalties) and section 5 (the office is not held responsible) are what let a pantry owner sign alone. Do not add fees, damages, exclusivity or minimums.

**Plan terms this page carries** (plan, Phase 1 "What they sign", legal table row "Participation agreement with offices", appendix E): commitments on both sides; receiving terms; the sealed-product promise; the office is not held responsible for employee use; no employee personal data; anonymous survey; either side may leave on seven days' notice; the office may decline to be named; a 12-month term that renews; no fees; a veto on any brand.

**1. What we bring.** Products from vetted emerging snack and drink brands, free, roughly monthly as brands are available (there is no set schedule). Each drop arrives on a Tuesday or Wednesday morning inside your receiving window, with a display card. After each drop we send you a summary of your team's feedback. We add to your pantry; we don't replace your current supplier.

**2. You choose.** You pick the categories you want. You may veto any brand, category or allergen at any time, and we check each product's label against your vetoes before offering it. For your first three drops, nothing comes unless you reply yes. After that, we email you each brand at least five business days ahead, and it comes unless you say no.

**3. What you do for each drop.** Put it on the shelf within one business day of delivery. Send us one photo of it on the shelf, and one more on day 3 or day 5. Post the survey link once in Slack or by email. Help us reach five or more survey responses (no one on your team has to answer). Give us a ten-minute debrief after the drop. Tell us how many people are usually in on a Tuesday.

**4. Product standard.** Only individually packaged, sealed, shelf-stable products, commercially labeled with ingredients and allergens. Never alcohol, cannabis, CBD or THC, dietary supplements, refrigerated or perishable products, or products with health claims. We never open a package or change its label, and every display card repeats the product's allergen statement. Each brand must show us product liability insurance of at least $1 million before it ships, and we will carry general liability insurance before your first drop.

**5. Your team.** Employees take products only if they want to, and we will not hold you responsible for how anyone uses them. If anyone reports a problem with a product, tell us: we remove it from your pantry right away and tell the brand the same day. We collect no personal data about your employees, except an email address an employee chooses to give for a brand's newsletter. Otherwise the survey is anonymous.

**6. Photos and your name.** We photograph only the shelf and display (never people, screens or whiteboards). We may share those photos with brands, and use them in our own materials without anything that identifies your office. We give a brand your company name and address only after it signs with us, only to run its campaign. It must keep them confidential and may not approach you to arrange placements directly for six months. Anywhere else, we name you only if you tick a box here: ☐ on our founding roster, which brands see ☐ publicly, for example on our website.

**7. No money, no penalties.** Neither side pays the other anything. You will never owe us money or damages under this agreement. If something here stops working for you, tell us; either of us may simply end participation.

**8. Term and leaving.** This runs for 12 months from signing and renews each year. Either of us may end it at any time with seven days' notice by email. Leftover product at the end of a drop: we collect it or leave it with you to keep, as the brand chooses, and we tell you which before the drop. You never store anything for us beyond the drop.

**9. New company.** If the Company's New York registration is not complete when this is signed, [FOUNDER NAME] signs for it. Once it is registered, the Company takes over this agreement in [FOUNDER NAME]'s place, and you agree to that now. We will email you when it happens. Nothing else changes for you.

**10. The whole agreement.** This page and your intake form (preferences, vetoes, headcount) are the whole agreement. Changes need an email from each side. New York law applies. Electronic signatures count. The person signing for the office runs its pantry and can agree to this for the office.
