When the card is full: the give-way order
The template fits the card itself. If text does not fit, it gives way in this order and tells you on screen and in the render output:
(1) a brand logo shrinks from 0.9 to 0.55 in; (2) the two sentences shrink from 15 to 12 pt; (3) spacing tightens; (4) the find-it
line goes from 11 to 10 pt; (5) the QR shrinks from 1.45 to 1.15 in; (6) a two-line brand name goes back to one condensed line
if it can; (7) the product line is left off; (8) the brand name shrinks toward 36 pt, widening again where it can; (9) the two
sentences are left off. The name, the thing the wide photo depends on, gives way after the product line (review round 1).
Long ingredient lists. For a package whose only allergen declaration is its ingredient list, a list too long to share a face with
the story and QR moves to a face of its own, and that face is the front: the brand name (43 to 36 pt), the whole list and "may
contain" line at 14 pt, and where to buy. It is marked "this side faces the room" beside the card ID. The other face carries the
story, "Take one" and the QR, and its panel points to the list. If the list is still too long, the "Take one" sentence and then the
buy line leave the front (both are on the other face). If it still does not fit, the card prints a red DO NOT PLACE panel and the
render command writes no PDF. The allergen panel never changes.
