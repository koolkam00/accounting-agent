Set up instant advertising with custom table tents.

  • 16pt premium thickness available
  • 5" x 7" size
  • Glossy & matte paper stock options
  • Folded, self-standing design
  • Full-color printing on the outside
  • Customizable designs, including QR code options

Mini billboards for a variety of uses

Looking for an easy, effective way to greet customers, promote your offerings or share essential information? Table tents will rise to the challenge – and stay standing on their own – with easy setup. You can use them to display important information, like promotions, menu items, specials or service lists. And when you add a QR code, you can easily share even more information by linking to your menu, map, website and more.

Easy to customize & set up

Custom table tents are a great way to greet and guide customers at trade shows, reception areas and lobbies. You can also use them for personal events, like weddings and baby showers. With its double-sided design, you can add extra information on the back or print the same design on both sides for 360-degree visibility. Your printed table tents will arrive flat and unfolded. They have 2 flaps at the bottom that interlock for a quick and easy setup.

Intuitive design process

Ready to create your table top tents? First, browse our customizable templates or upload your own design. For the best quality print, make sure you use high-resolution images and that your designs are clear and vibrant. Next, you can add your logo, messaging, images and a QR code, if you’d like. When your design is ready, we’ll take it from there, and your custom table tents will arrive ready to set up and show off.

Frequently asked questions

Both matte and glossy table tents are available in our 14pt standard and 16pt premium paper stocks.

At this time, our tents are only available in a 5" x 7" size in vertical orientation.

Your table tents will be your choice of glossy or matte on the outer, customer-facing sides and uncoated on the inner, unprinted sides.

Yes. You can upload your own artwork, or design your table tent in our online studio.

As you design your table tents, you’ll be able to add a QR code by clicking "QR Codes" on the left side of the design studio. Then, type in your URL in the specified field and click "Add QR Code" to generate the code. The QR code will automatically appear on your design, and you can move and resize it to your liking. You can use this feature to link to your website, socials, pinned location, online menu and more.

If you’d like even more customization options, you can also use our free QR code generator, download the generated QR code as an image file and upload it to your table tent design.

Our table tents have 2 flaps that interlock at the bottom, to make them stand on their own. Please note that they will arrive flat and unfolded.
