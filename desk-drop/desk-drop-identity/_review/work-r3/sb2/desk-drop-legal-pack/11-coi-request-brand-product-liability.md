# 11 · Certificate of insurance request to a brand: product liability, with us as additional insured

Version 0.7 · 2026-10-24

**The company in this file.** The company's legal name is set once, in the `[COMPANY NAME: …]` slot on the "Additional insured" line of the block to forward. Replace it with the name on the filed Articles of Organization, letter for letter, including "LLC".

**What it is for.** Product liability sits with the brand (plan, legal table). Before a brand's product ships, it must show product liability coverage of at least \$1,000,000 and add the company as an additional insured (plan; brand agreement file 10, section 2(c)). This file is the request the founder sends, the version the brand forwards to its broker, a reminder, and the check to run when the certificate arrives.

**When it is used.** In the "close and collect" email on the brand's yes, with the signed agreement and invoice (plan, Phase 2 step 4). It must be checked and filed before the ship-by date (T−10). For campaign zero, ship-by is Tuesday 2026-12-22 (derived, founder to confirm), so send the request no later than Tuesday 2026-12-08.

**How to fill it in.** Replace the brackets. If the company is not yet formed, do not send this request yet: the brand agreement is signed only after formation (file 10 notes).

## Block to forward · For the brand's insurance broker or agent

> **Request for a certificate of insurance and additional-insured endorsement**
>
> **Named insured:** [BRAND LEGAL NAME], [BRAND ADDRESS]
>
> **Additional insured (vendor):** [COMPANY NAME: Desk Drop LLC], [POSTAL ADDRESS]
>
> **Certificate holder:** the additional insured above, same address.
>
> **Products:** [PRODUCT NAME AND SKU], or all of [BRAND]'s products.
>
> **Coverage to show:** commercial general liability including products-completed operations, or a separate product liability policy, with at least \$1,000,000 each occurrence. Please show the products-completed operations aggregate, and whether the policy is written on an occurrence or claims-made basis. If claims-made, please state the retroactive date.
>
> **Additional-insured status:** as a vendor, for "bodily injury" or "property damage" arising out of [BRAND]'s products that the additional insured distributes. ISO form CG 20 15 (Additional Insured – Vendors), or your policy's blanket vendors endorsement, is fine. Our signed campaign agreement with [BRAND], dated [AGREEMENT DATE], requires this coverage, if your blanket endorsement needs a written contract.
>
> **Period:** our agreement asks for this coverage, with the additional insured above, from [SHIP-BY DATE] until [COVER-UNTIL DATE: 12 months after WINDOW CLOSE DATE, or the latest best-by date on the units if later], because units given away in the campaign stay in office pantries and go home with employees after it ends. If the policy expires before that date, please send the renewal certificate and endorsement when it renews. If the policy is claims-made, its retroactive date must be on or before [SHIP-BY DATE], and the coverage must stay in force, or an extended reporting period be bought, through [COVER-UNTIL DATE].
>
> **Please send:** the certificate on your standard form (ACORD 25 is fine) and a copy of the endorsement that adds the additional insured. We are not asking for any wording on the certificate that the policy does not contain.
>
> **What the additional insured does with the product, so you can check the vendors endorsement fits:** it receives sealed, individually packaged, commercially labeled units; stores the sealed cases at room temperature, clean, dry, off the floor, away from heat and as the label directs, from arrival until the campaign ends (usually two to three weeks, up to about four around the December holidays; leftover units the brand asks back can wait up to ten more business days for its return label, so up to about six weeks in all); moves sealed units, unopened, from [BRAND]'s shipping cartons into one delivery box per office; and places them in office pantries, free to employees, next to a separate display card that repeats [BRAND]'s own text and allergen line. It never opens, alters, repacks or relabels a unit, puts nothing on a unit, and does no demonstrations or sampling cups. If moving sealed units between shipping cartons would fall under the endorsement's repackaging exclusion, please tell us and we will deliver in [BRAND]'s original cartons instead.
>
> **Send to:** [FOUNDER NAME], [EMAIL]

If the certificate has not arrived by T−10, the product does not ship until it does (brand agreement, section 2(c)). If that means the product cannot arrive by T−3, section 8 moves the drop one week; tell every office the same day. If the product then misses the new date's T−3, section 8 lets either side treat the campaign as cancelled by the brand. The plan's rule stands throughout: a brand that cannot produce a certificate is not qualified.

## Check when the certificate arrives (five minutes)

Tick every line before confirming the ship date. If any line fails, reply to the broker with the line that failed.

- [ ] **Named insured** matches the brand's legal name in the agreement (file 10, Part 1).
- [ ] The **each-occurrence** limit on the policy that carries products liability (general liability, or a separate product liability policy) is \$1,000,000 or more, and a **products-completed operations aggregate** is shown.
- [ ] **Policy dates** cover the ship-by date through the window close date. If the policy ends before [COVER-UNTIL DATE] (12 months after window close, or the latest best-by date if later), put its expiry date in the calendar and ask the broker for the renewal certificate and endorsement then (record it as `coi_policy_end_date`, a suggested paid-brands column).
- [ ] The policy is marked **occurrence**. If it is marked claims-made, it is still usable for now if the retroactive date is on or before the ship-by date; ask the broker to confirm that date in writing, and note it for the attorney hour (file 08, item 16).
- [ ] The **"ADDL INSD"** column is marked on the line that carries products liability (general liability, or a separate product liability policy under "other"), and the description names our company as additional insured.
- [ ] A **copy of the endorsement** is attached: either it names our company in its schedule, or it is blanket vendors wording that covers anyone the brand agreed in a written contract to add.
- [ ] **Our name** on the certificate and endorsement matches the Articles of Organization exactly.
- [ ] The certificate holder is our company at [POSTAL ADDRESS].

Then save the certificate and endorsement as `[BRAND]-COI-[YYYY-MM-DD].pdf` in the campaign folder and record `coi_received_date` in the paid-brands row (CONTRACTS 6.4).

**Why the endorsement matters more than the certificate.** The ACORD 25 certificate itself says it "is issued as a matter of information only and confers no rights upon the certificate holder" and that if the holder is an additional insured, "the policy(ies) must be endorsed." The endorsement is what actually adds us.

**Why we keep units sealed and unlabeled.** The ISO vendors endorsement excludes, among other things, any "express warranty unauthorized by" the brand, any "physical or chemical change in the product made intentionally by the vendor", "repackaging" (with narrow exceptions), products "labeled or relabeled" by or for the vendor, demonstrations, and the vendor's own sole negligence. Our handling rules (file 10, section 5) and the card using only the brand's own words are written to stay outside those exclusions; the brand's broker confirms the carton point (block above). Our own general liability policy (quote script in file 06; building certificates in file 12) is what responds to our own mistakes.

**What we deliberately do not ask for.** Primary and non-contributory wording, waiver of subrogation and notice-of-cancellation endorsements. They can cost a small brand time or money, and the plan does not require them. Ask the attorney whether to add them once the business runs more than a few campaigns.
