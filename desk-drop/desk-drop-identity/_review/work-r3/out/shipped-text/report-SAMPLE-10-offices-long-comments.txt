Engagement: 103 QR scans across 10 offices · 14 code redemptions to Fri 2027-02-19, per the brand (day-30 check Thu 2027-03-11).

What we learned
SAMPLE Offices F and H ran out before day 5 even after top-ups; size
units to Tuesday headcount more closely.
SAMPLE Seven people said it was gone by the time they looked, so
supply, not interest, limited trial in two offices.

Recommendation
SAMPLE Renew into offices A, F and H
with 20% more units each, and add two
new offices. Keep the card; add "three
questions, 20 seconds" beside the QR.

SAMPLE Some people read the QR as a reorder link; the card could say
"three questions, 20 seconds".
Limitations. Each number is a separate step, counted as stated. Delivered: our count at handoff. Placed: units visible in the placement photo; an office may
hold some back for later. Depleted: from photos or the pantry owner's count by day 5; one person may take several, and sharing and disposal are invisible.
Tuesday headcount: a range the office reports; not everyone visits the pantry. Survey responses: anonymous and self-selected, from small samples, so one
answer can move a share by several points. A QR scan is curiosity, not intent. Code redemptions: reported by the brand; some of those people would have
bought anyway. Retailer sales, where shown, are confounded by everything else that happened that month and are suggestive only. Incremental purchases were
not measured, so this report does not show whether the campaign caused any sale.
