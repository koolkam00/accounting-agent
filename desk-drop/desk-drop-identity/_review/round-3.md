## 4. The four reviewers in the brief

- **Packaging designer who has watched products die on pantry shelves.** The card does its job. The cobalt band and a 39 to 70 pt name survive 120 px in every variant I made, including a 33-character name over a 25-word ALL-CAPS panel. "Take one." and a 1.15 to 1.45 in QR read from across a room. Faults: a wide logo can now block a card that the typed name would carry (R3-04), and the long-ingredient card hides the QR from the room (R3-06). Still open from earlier rounds, and rightly left to the real-stock shelf test: the A-frame has no lock tab, and the standing footprint is about 7 x 4 in.
- **Food-safety inspector reading the allergen line.** The core is excellent: 14 pt, weight 600, pure black, bordered, verbatim-checked, on both faces, enforced in code, with two flavors each under its own package's name and no "no allergens" option. The fault is the claims net: a card can print "maintains bowel regularity and promotes digestion" with no warning (R3-02). The 9 pt "Always check the package" line contradicts the system's own floor (R3-09).
- **Pantry owner deciding shelf or drawer.** The office one-pager is short, honest and complete: what you get, what we ask (two photos, one post, five responses, debrief, headcount), never asked, privacy, veto, a week's notice and the drop week. The fixed pitch line says "one photo" while "What we ask" says two. That comes from the plan's fixed pitch, so it belongs in REPORT.md's plan conflicts (R3-01), not in a rewrite.
- **Brand founder deciding whether to pay $600.** The founding proposal is clear and matches the agreement. It prints every date from the drop day, the cancel-by date and the per-office refund, and says "No sales guarantee". The mock page shows this proposal's own offices under the "Illustrative placeholders, not results" band. The report they would receive is honest rung by rung. Faults: a standard proposal for a brand on four real cluster shelves cannot be produced (R3-03), and a founder could type "sales rose 20%" into their report unchallenged (R3-02).
