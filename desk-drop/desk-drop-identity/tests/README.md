# Tests

Run everything with `bash tests/run-all.sh` from the `desk-drop-identity` folder (Node 18+, Playwright, ImageMagick, poppler's pdftotext, pdfinfo and pdftoppm). The visual tests write images for a person to look at; I looked at every one listed here. Results below are from 2026-10-24 after review round 3 (v1.4): **59 of 59 suite steps pass** in 3 min 46 s (with `DD_QR_DECODER` set to a folder with jsqr 1.4.0 and pngjs 7.0.0; 58 plus one skip without it). It was 47 after review round 2 and 37 after round 1. The documents step (`tests/documents/doc-tests.js`) runs 98 checks, all passing (89 after round 2). The new claims step (`tests/claims/claims-test.js`) runs 286 phrase checks: 69 claims caught, 39 ordinary phrases passed, 4 nutrient lines warned, 174 shipped sentences passed. `tests/details/details-test.js` runs 9. `render/render-page.js` now refuses a spec page whose text would run past its footer or edge, so `build-pdfs.sh` stops instead of shipping a cut-off spec.

## Files

- `run-all.sh`: the whole suite. `check-tokens.js`: token and contrast check.
- `photo-sim/`: wide and close photo simulation, allergen size sweep, outputs in `photo-sim/out/`.
- `downscale/`: 120 px and 60 px contact sheets.
- `name-length/`: rename test and its images.
- `guide-walkthrough/`: the stranger test (`walkthrough.js`), the swap test (`swap-test.js`, `swap-*.png`, `swap-result.json`) and their screenshots.
- `claims/`: `claims-test.js` (v1.4): every claim phrase, ordinary phrase, nutrient line and shipped sentence with its expected result.
- `documents/`: `doc-tests.js` (98 checks on the documents, signature, avatar and legal versions), `limitations-frozen.txt`, and `cases/` (stress inputs: proposals with a landscape map for 5 to 12 offices, including since v1.4 the round-3 reviewer's real store sets and a must-refuse case, reports for 10 to 12 offices with long text, brand one-pager roster lines; several are the round-1 reviewer's own files).
- `panel/`: `records-test.js` and its screenshot (pick, add and remove records).
- `details/`: `details-test.js` and its screenshot (your details in Chrome alone, then a rename to "Shelfwise" and a newer value typed into `brand.json`).
- `proposal-map/`: B's portrait map test, our own SAMPLE landscape map, B's SAMPLE brand map (copied from `cluster-1-flatiron/brand-maps/`), and `B-SAMPLE-long-names-map.png`, drawn by B's own `_build/public_map.py` on a scratch copy of B's folder (stores B-R-017, B-R-020, B-R-018, B-R-016, the four longest public names in B's `retailers.csv`).

## Limits of these tests

The swap-test time is a model, not a stopwatch on a person. Since v1.3 a "Quick card" box opens guide section 2: a repeat user reads its five lines (about 30 seconds) instead of the whole section (about 3 minutes by the model). The first real person to make a card should time it and write the minutes into this file.


The photos are simulations, not photographs of a printed card on a real shelf. Before the first drop, print one card on the real stock, stand it in a real pantry with real product, take both photos with the phone you will use, and check the same three things (name from across the room, allergen panel when zoomed, QR scans). The plan already asks for this test on a real shelf the week before campaign zero.
