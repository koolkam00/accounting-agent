**Operations notes for F**
- **A claims error is a stop. Show it to the founder.** Since v1.3 the tent card, the report and every document share one claims list (health claims, structure or function wording such as "supports gut health", "guaranteed", sales lift, ROI, conversions, acquisition, "new customers"), widened in v1.4 ("builds strong bones", "promotes digestion", "good for your gut", "best", "sales rose", "converted", "uplift", "3x return"). A hit is an error: exit 2, no PDF, an `errors[]` entry starting "Claims check". Never set `claims_reviewed` yourself; only the founder does, with a reason.
- **The check is a net, not a review.** Exit 0 does not mean the text is claim-free. The founder reads `card_sentences`, `product_name` and `brand_name` before every card print, and `learned`, `recommendation` and `retailer_velocity` before every report. The Chrome panels have a tick box for each read; the command line cannot tick, so F's kitting and report checklists carry the same two reads as steps.
- A card warning "The brand's logo was replaced by the typed name" is fine to print; tell the founder, since the brand sent the logo. `fit[i].logo` reads `logo-wide-replaced-by-typed-name`.
- `fit[i].qr_off_room_face = true` (a long ingredient list, v1.4): the QR is only on the face toward the product. F's tracker should mark that office so the founder confirms its Slack or email survey post went out, and the report's "What we learned" says the card had its QR on the back. With `qr_on_room_face = true` both faces carry the QR.
- A two-flavor card that does not fit at 14 pt is refused with "one card per flavor": make two records with the same `office_id` and `qr_url`, one flavor each. `fit[i].flavors` is 1 or 2.
- Verify `allergen_line_verbatim` against the physical package at receiving (T minus 5). The Chrome panel asks for this tick; the command line cannot, so F's kitting checklist should.
- A card warning containing "left off", "too long to share a face" or "other side" means the card changed shape; show it to the founder. Since v1.4 the Chrome panel says "Ready to print" with such notes listed under it, so a ready card can still carry notes: read them. `fit[i].ingredients_on_front_face = true` means the front carries the whole ingredient list and is marked "this side faces the room": the kitting checklist should say so for that office.
- Messages start with "Card N: " (or "Label N: ") only when a file holds more than one record.
- A report warning "under the five the office committed to" is a fact for the debrief, not an error.
- Photos must show no identifiable people; the Chrome panel asks for this tick before printing the report.
