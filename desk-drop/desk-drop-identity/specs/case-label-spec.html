Case label: print spec

Template v1.0 · 2026-10-23 · file: templates/case-label/case-label.html

Size8.5 x 5.5 in (half a Letter sheet), landscape. Two labels per sheet.
StockHalf-sheet shipping labels in the Avery 5126 size (5-1/2 x 8-1/2 in, 2 per sheet; Avery template page, opened 2026-10-23), or any brand with the same layout. Or plain paper: cut on the dashed line and tape the label flat on the case with clear packing tape over all four edges.
Safe area0.4 in from every label edge. Home printers cannot print the outer 0.25 in or so.
BleedNone. Nothing touches the edge.
Print settingsPaper Letter, Scale 100 (Actual size), Margins Default, Background graphics on.
ColorInk on white. One cobalt band (office labels) or ink band (reserve labels). Prints fine in black and white.
Half-used sheetPut {"_skip": true} as the first item in the JSON to start on the bottom label.
Where it goesOne label on the top of each case. If cases will be stacked, print a second copy for one side. Every case for an office gets its own label: "Case 1 of 2", "Case 2 of 2".

Rules

  • Count the units as you pack and write the count you packed, not the count you planned.
  • Labels carry business details only: office, address, the pantry owner's name, brand, counts, IDs, the founder's business phone. No employee details.
  • The reserve label stays with you. Reserve units are for day-3 top-ups and never stay at an office.
