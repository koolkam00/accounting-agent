Your contact details

Every document, label and card reads your details from one file, system/brand.js. Fill them in here once, in Chrome. No terminal needed.

How it works

  1. Type your details in the boxes below. The signature on the right updates as you type.
  2. Press Save brand.js. Chrome saves a file called brand.js in your Downloads folder (if Chrome names it brand (1).js, rename it brand.js).
  3. Move that file into the desk-drop-identity/system/ folder and choose Replace.
  4. Reload this page: the status box says All your details are filled in. Reload any open template: your details print and are no longer yellow.
  5. Copy the signature into Gmail (guide section 13).
