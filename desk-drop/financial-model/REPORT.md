## What was built (five lines)

1. One workbook, `financial-model/Desk Drop financial model v1.xlsx`, 21 tabs on one shared drop engine, built to open in Google Sheets with formulas intact: no macros, scripts or add-ons, and no numeric literal other than 0 or 1 in any of its 20,306 formulas. Every INDEX over a single row names its row (INDEX(range, 1, column)), the form Google documents. Every assumption sits in one labeled cell on Inputs with its unit, source, plan default and a 'changed' flag; the company name sits in one cell.
2. An editable drop schedule (five derived drops and seven spare rows) feeds a per-drop P&L with contribution per founder hour, a 26-week cash flow with startup outlays at their timing and brand cash on the plan's terms, a monthly steady state, break-even, four plain-formula sensitivity tables and the plan's capital table reconciled. Overhead is paid once; per-drop allocations are views of it.
3. Live tabs for hours (by hand or from Toggl), pipeline events and campaign actuals switch each drop from plan to actual; Checks flags any pasted date or amount stored as text, and the Dashboard banner turns red. A gate dashboard shows every threshold beside its live value as pass, miss or not yet, plus the decision rule, the scenario the numbers point to, the gap to $6,510 and, for each gate, what you have paid out, the refunds you would owe and the net if you stop there. Scenarios A to D are selectable assumption sets; any lever a scenario does not name follows the plan base.
4. The plan's five scenario rows, its founder-hour table cell by cell, the monthly picture, the $47 line and the $3,360 to $6,510 capital total reproduce within rounding (Plan_Check). 38 checks pass (plus one information row) and 151 behaviour tests pass on edited copies, including every edge case the brief names.
5. At plan defaults: lowest cumulative cash -$4,476 in week 13 (Mon 2027-01-18), with the $350 trademark at Gate 2; capital to commit $5,476, $1,034 under the plan's $6,510, and 5 unpriced cost lines still count as $0; standard runs to Gate 3 earn $61.3 per founder hour ($51.4 with a roster big enough for one drop per office a month); steady state $7,660 a month at $81.1 an hour. ## How to use it (five lines)

1. In Google Sheets, open a blank spreadsheet, then File, Import, pick the .xlsx, 'Replace spreadsheet'; set the locale to United States and the time zone to America/New_York, and check that the Dashboard says 'all integrity and data checks pass' (README.md has the steps; this needs your Google login).
2. Every week: add hours on Hours_Log, paste or type pipeline events on Pipeline, read the Dashboard top to bottom. Optionally type your bank balance on Cash_Flow, row 30.
3. After each drop: paste the operations exports into Actuals_Campaigns and Actuals_DropCosts (exactly the HANDOFF.md columns, dates as real dates and amounts as numbers), then set 'Actuals in' to Y for that drop on Schedule once revenue, payments, costs and hours are final (by T+10). Day-30 redemptions and renewal answers can follow later. If the banner turns red, open Checks first.
4. To change an assumption, type in its yellow or blue cell on Inputs or Schedule. To test a price, change Inputs section 3, 'Standard price per office', and read 'Price in force' and 'A standard drop at that price' on the Dashboard; the Calculator tests one drop without moving the model.
5. To see a decision situation, pick A to D on Inputs section 2. On the decision date, read 'The rule says' and 'Scenario the real numbers point to'.
