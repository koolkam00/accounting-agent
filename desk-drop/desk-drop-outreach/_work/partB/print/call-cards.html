Open (30 s)

Who you are; testing an idea (a week in a handful of New York offices, photos and feedback, near their stores); not asking them to buy today; a price question is coming, and their number comes first; seven questions, about 25 minutes.

The anchor rule

Ask Q4, then wait. "I'll tell you, but I'd like your number first." Write it. Then: "For reference, the standard price I'm testing is $1,500 for ten offices. How does that land?" Founding price only if asked: five offices for $600, paid up front, $120 an office for the next two campaigns; in return, if it runs as promised, a short note from them that we can publish with a case study.

Gate 0 counts this brand only if

the higher of the two numbers is $500 or more and the speaker controls the budget alone. A later call with no budget control or a lower number takes it back.

Weeks 4 to 7: the seven checks

1 sealed, shelf-stable, 60+ days at the drop · 2 ingredients and nine allergens on the label · 3 store within ten blocks or online code · 4 ship 275 units to one Manhattan address in ten business days · 5 product liability of $1M per occurrence, us as additional insured · 6 approves $600 alone · 7 why New York now. Excluded: alcohol, cannabis/CBD/THC, supplements, refrigerated, preparation, health claims.

Say only what is true today

No offices signed until the tracker says so; no results yet ("I'd rather tell you that than make up a number"); no sales guarantee; no free trial; no employee data.
