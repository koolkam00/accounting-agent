Our story

From 88 acres to every table

A farm kid, a fourth date that ended in the ER, and a basement kitchen full of seeds. This is how 88 Acres® began.

Shop best sellersStart the story

Our story so far

  1. 01

    North Brookfield, Massachusetts

    The farm

    Co-founder Nicole Ledoux grew up on an 88-acre farm where her family grew produce and raised animals. Her first business? Selling the farm's goods at the family farmstand. That's where her love of food began, and where we got our name. The barn up top is hers.

  2. 02

    Boston · December 2010

    The fourth date

    After a decade in finance, Nicole met Rob on Match.com. On their fourth date, his dinner was cross-contaminated with nuts, and he's severely allergic.

    “This ended up with him in the ER and me in the kitchen.”Nicole Ledoux, co-founder
  3. 03

    2012 · Training for triathlons

    The basement kitchen

    The bars Nicole packed for training were full of nuts, and the nut-free ones she found just didn't taste good. So she baked her own with seeds, in their basement apartment. Soon their Boston workout crew was asking for bars before every ride.

  4. 04

    Dorchester, MA · 2015

    Our own bakery

    No co-packer could bake bars free from the top 9 food allergens, so we built our own bakery. A Kickstarter pre-sold our first run in two days. We put the bakery in a neighborhood that needed jobs, with help from a local economic development group and a food startup accelerator.

  5. 05

    March 2015

    Launch day

    We launched with three seed bars: Triple Berry Crumble, Dark Chocolate Sea Salt and Apple Ginger Crisp. Two of them are still best sellers. Our first store was Whole Foods Market in New England, and we pitched with bars in ziplock bags.

    Seed butters came next, after Rob admitted he'd never tasted nut butter. The first batches went into mason jars.

  6. 06

    Canton, Massachusetts · 2023

    A bigger home

    We moved into a larger bakery we built in Canton. Everything is still handmade in small batches at our dedicated allergen-free bakery.

    Go inside our bakery
  7. 07

    Today · you are here

    Welcome to the table

    Seed + Oat Bars, Protein Bars, seed butters and Seed'nola, all free from the top 9 food allergens. You'll even find our bars on Delta flights.
