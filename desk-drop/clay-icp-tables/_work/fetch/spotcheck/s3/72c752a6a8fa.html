Questions
Orders
I've made an order mistake, what can I do?

We do not make changes to flavors or quantities after an order is placed. We will try our best to change addresses or add gift notes after an order is placed. Email us at orders@springandmulberry with all of the information that needs to be changed and we'll respond within 24 hours.

When will my order arrive?

We ship via UPS, FedEx, and USPS. Our standard ship times vary by delivery zip code and package weight, but the longest time will be 5 days in cold weather months and 3 days in warm weather months. When your order ships, we’ll send you a tracking number. You’ll also receive a confirmation email when your package is out for delivery and when it arrives. We know that is a lot of emails, but we want to ensure your chocolate does not melt or fall victim to porch pirates.

My order did not arrive, help!

If you've signed up for Route package protection, please file a claim through the email they sent with your order. Otherwise, please email us at orders@springandmulberry.com and we'll help you!

How do I make changes to my subscription?

You can modify, pause, or cancel your subsciption in the customer portal, which is accessed via the link your receive in each subscription email.

When will my next subscription ship?

You will receive an email three days before your next automated shipment. In that email, you will find a link to modify, pause, or cancel your subscription.

What is your refund policy?

We offer refunds or reshipments for products that have melted in transit. Please email us photos of your product to hello@springandmulberry.com. We do not offer refunds for any other reason.

Do you take returns or exchanges?

Unfortunately, not – because our products are perishable and require proper storage, we cannot accept returns. If there are any problems with your order, please reach out at hello@springandmulberry.com so that we can assist you.

Do you ship outside of the continental US?

We currently only ship within the continental U.S. We can not ship to Canada, Europe, or Australia due to chocolate metling in transit while waiting in customs.

Warm Weather Shipping
What are your policies for warm weather shipping?

In warm weather months, we ship Mondays and Wednesdays with express shipping using ice packs and insulation.

My ice pack arrived warm, is that ok?

Yes, that’s to be expected, as ice packs start to melt as soon as they leave our packing team. As long as your chocolate is not melted, it is fine that your ice packs are no longer frozen.

Where do you ship in warm weather months?

Things heat up from June–September! During the summer months, orders shipping to areas experiencing extreme temperatures (Arizona, Texas, or Florida) may be temporarily delayed. We’ll keep an eye on the weather and ship your chocolate during a cooler window to help ensure it arrives in the best possible condition.

How do I ensure my chocolate doesn’t melt in transit?

The most common reason chocolate melts in transit is that it is left outside in direct sun. The best way to prevent this is to bring your package inside within an hour of delivery.

My chocolate melted, what can I do?

If your order melted in transit, please get in touch at hello@springandmulberry.com within 5 days of delivery with photos and we’ll do our best to make it right.

Recall
Why did the recall get expanded?

Food safety investigations, especially in foods like chocolate, can take time.

In low-moisture foods, contamination can be difficult to detect and isolate, even with repeated testing. Since January, we have been working continuously with our manufacturing partners to conduct a comprehensive root cause investigation, in coordination with leading food safety experts, and the U.S. Food and Drug Administration.

Over several months, extensive testing was conducted across ingredients, finished products, and the production environment, with results consistently negative. A specific lot of date ingredient used in the production of our base chocolate has now been identified as the most likely source of contamination. 

Out of an abundance of caution, we are expanding the recall to include all chocolate bars produced with this specific lot of date powder.

How can we be sure the product is safe going forward?

We’ve identified the likely source of the issue and removed that ingredient from our supply chain.

We’ve also implemented additional supplier controls, testing protocols, and verification steps for future production. We continue to work closely with food safety experts and our manufacturing partners to ensure these measures are strong and effective.

As always, the safety of our customers comes first, and we will continue to take every step necessary to ensure the integrity of our products.

Should I be worried if I already ate the chocolate?

If you’ve already consumed the product and are not experiencing symptoms, there is no need for concern.

All products included in this expanded recall have tested negative for Salmonella, and there have been no confirmed reports of illness associated with these products.

If you are feeling unwell, we recommend contacting your healthcare provider.

Should we be concerned about dates in general being contaminated?

No. This investigation identified a specific lot of date powder as the likely source of contamination—not dates broadly.

Dates are widely used across the food industry and are generally considered a safe ingredient. Like any agricultural product, however, ingredients can occasionally become contaminated somewhere within the supply chain.

We’ve removed the affected ingredient lot from our supply chain and implemented additional supplier controls and testing protocols moving forward.

Do you make your chocolate yourselves?

Like most food brands, we work with a network of specialized manufacturing and ingredient partners to produce our products safely and at scale.

We partner with highly reputable companies, including one of the oldest and most established chocolate manufacturers in the United States and the country’s largest organic date cooperative. These partners supply products to retailers nationwide and operate under strict food safety requirements, including FDA Good Manufacturing Practices.

Throughout production, ingredients and finished products undergo multiple layers of food safety testing and verification.

How can I be sure products are safe now?

Through our investigation, we identified the likely root cause of the recall as a specific lot of date ingredient used in the production of our base chocolate. This was identified through extensive testing, product tracing, and genome sequencing analysis.

All products currently shipping were made using different ingredient lots and have undergone significant additional testing and review prior to release.

When will you be shipping again?

We are currently shipping orders. All products currently available on our website were made using a different lot of date ingredient than the one identified as the likely root cause of the recall. All ingredients and finished products available for sale have undergone significant additional testing prior to release.

When will products be back in stock?

We’re working hard to bring all products back as quickly as possible, but we don’t yet have exact timing for every flavor. Some products may return sooner than others as we carefully rebuild inventory using newly sourced ingredients and additional safety protocols. We will email customers as flavors return. We truly appreciate your patience and support during this process.

Who can I contact for more information?

Please contact us at recalls@springandmulberry.com for more information.

Product Issues & Storage
I'm unhappy with the amount of toppings on my chocolate, where can I share feedback?

Our chocolate bars are hand-topped, which creates a natural fluctuation in the amount and spread of the toppings. It is the beauty and frustration of an artisanal product. If you are unhappy, please email us at orders@springandmulberry.com with the details of your concern. Please include batch numbers (located under the best buy date) and photos if possible, and we'll provide a partial refund if the bars fall outside of our guidelines.

I experienced a health or safety concern with your chocolate, who can I speak with?

We work with globally sourced agriculture products that can mold, contain pit fragments, or foreign bodies from time to time. We are so sorry if you've had an issue and we'll do what we can to make it right. Please email us at orders@springandmulberry.com to share your experiene. Please include batch numbers (located under the best buy date) and photos if possible, and we'll get back to you with a resolution within 1 business day.

My chocolate looks chalky, is that ok?

If your chocolate has a chalky or white appearence, it means that it melted and then hardened again. That is why it is important to keep the chocolate out of the sun and heat. If your chocolate is white/chalky upon delivery, we will replace your order.

My chocolate looks like it shrunk, is that ok?

If your chocolate has shrunk inside the unsealed packaging, it means that it melte. If your chocolate has shunk upon delivery, we will replace your order.

What is the shelf life of your products?

The best buy date is located on the back of each bar. For our Embellished Bars with toppings, the best buy date is 12 months and for our Essence Line with essential oils it is 14-16 months. Please note that our chocolate is delcious for 2-3 months beyond the best buy date.

How should I store my chocolate?

Store your chocolate in a cool, dry place such as a kitchen pantry. Our chocolate does not contain stabilizers and so it can melt above 75F.

Can I refrigerate or freeze my chocolate?

We do not recommend refrigerating or freezing our chocolate, as it can cause the chocolate to bloom.


Our dark chocolate is sweetened exclusively with dates, which have a higher moisture content than sugar. As a result, the chocolate can bloom in the refrigerator or freezer, even when stored for short periods of time.


Blooming is caused by the sugars of the dates crystalizing, leading to a speckled appearance on the surface of the chocolate. Bloom is unsightly but does not harm the flavor or shelf life of the chocolate.

Ingredients & Allergens
Are your products organic?

We eat organically and believe it is important for the health of our bodies and the planet. We use organic ingredients whenever possible. Our Essence Line is 100% organic and our Embellished line is 80% organic. Please see the back of each bar for a complete list of organic ingredients.

Are your products vegan or plant-based?

All of our chocolate bars are plant-based and all flavors are vegan with the exception of the Lavender, Bee Pollen, Rose Petal Bar. Bee pollen is the byproduct of a worker honeybee, therefore making it an animal-derived food.


We do not use any milk products in the production of our chocolate, although the facility that produces our chocolate manufactures products using milk chocolate.

Are your products gluten-free?

Our products are naturally gluten-free, but are produced on equipment that also processes wheat products. That means there may be traces of gluten.

Are your products nut-free?

The only chocolate bar that directly contains nuts is our Medjool Date, Pecan, Himalayan Salt Bar. All other products do not directly contain nuts, however, our products are manufactured on equipment that processes peanuts and other tree nuts, so may contain traces.

What are the lead levels in your chocolate?

California's maximum allowable dose level (MADL) for lead is 0.5 micrograms per ounce. We test our cacao for lead with an independent third party. For this year's harvest the levels are .11 micrograms per ounce, 77.6% below the limit, which is considered very low.

What are the cadmium levels in your chocolate?

California's maximum allowable dose level (MADL) for cadmium is 4.1 micrograms per ounce. We test our cacao for cadmium with an independent third party. For this year's harvest the levels are .54 micrograms per ounce, 87.7% below the limit, which is considered very low.

Where do you source your cacao?

We source our cacao direct-trade from a single origin estate in West Africa where farmers employ organic and regenerative farming practices, and where they are paid a premium to fair-trade wages.

What kind of dates do you use?

We source our dates from one of the largest organic farming cooperatives in the Coachella Valley. The dates within our chocolate are dried and then ground before being added to the cacao butter.

More About Us
What's the story behind the name?

Spring & Mulberry is the intersection in New York City where Kathryn would meet her husband for dinner when they first started dating. Spring is also a time of rebirth, when plants and trees come to life, ultimately bearing fruits like mulberries that nourish our bodies and sweeten our lives.

What does the company stand for?

As a culture, we tend to think of sweet as one thing. A sugar-packed treat where we take a bite, and boom - we get the sugar high, but don't feel so great after.

We’re on a mission to transform America’s relationship with sugar by exploring a world of sweet centered around globally sourced, naturally derived, whole ingredients that offer nuanced sweetness and antioxidant packed nutrition.

Where are your products made?

All of our products and packaging are designed, developed, and made in America. Our company is based in Raleigh, North Carolina.

Is your packaging sustainable?

All of our packaging is recyclable or reusable, including the wrapper around the bar. We also seek to use modern, minimal design in our packaging so that each element employed is necessary for preservation or protection of the product.


All of our products and packaging are manufactured in the US, which reduces the carbon footprint caused by shipping abroad. It also supports American business.
