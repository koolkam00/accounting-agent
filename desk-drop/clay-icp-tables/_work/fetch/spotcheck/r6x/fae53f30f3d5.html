The Real Story

Can a snack change the world? We think so. Reelie was born from a belief that the foods we choose should do good for our bodies, our communities and the planet. What started as a family navigating health together became a bigger mission—to create snacks that nourish, connect and give back.


When Maca—daughter, sister, and cousin to many on our team—was diagnosed with an autoimmune condition, we took a closer look at what we were eating. With our family business already in the snack industry, we saw an opportunity to do better. Originally founded as Artisan Tropic, we evolved into Reelie— a name that reflects who we are today: Real People + Real Food = Reelie Good.

From Colombia to the U.S. with love

Rooted in the Tropics

A piece of our hearts still lives in Colombia, and we couldn’t resist sharing the goodness we grew up with, reimagined as crispy, crunchy snacks made from tropical ingredients we know and love. You don’t have to be Colombian to enjoy them — just a fan of great snacks. And because they’re made with better-for-you ingredients, they’re easy to share with everyone at the table.

From Our Family, To Yours

Everything we do—from how our ingredients are grown to how our snacks are made—is done with you in mind. So snack happy and feel Reelie good with every bite.

FROM FARM TO CHIP IN HOURS!

1. Seeded & Grown

Our plantains and cassava are grown in Colombia, using regenerative practices whenever possible — with a future vision to make our entire supply chain regenerative.

2. Harvest & Prepped

We harvest our crops and within hours, load them onto trucks and bring them to our Reelie facility in Colombia.

3. Farm to Chip

We go from farm to chip within hours of being harvested. With just some sea salt & sustainable palm fruit oil our Reelie delicious chips are created.

4. Sent with Love

Our chips are prepped, packed and sent with love for you to enjoy each delicious, crunchy bite.
