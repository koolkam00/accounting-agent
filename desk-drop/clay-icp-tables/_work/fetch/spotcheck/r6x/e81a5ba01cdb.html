FAQ’s

GENERAL QUESTIONS

Where are Firehook crackers made?

All Firehook crackers are baked in small batches in our own facility in Chantilly, Virginia.

Where can I find Firehook at a store near me?

Click here to find a retailer near you or to purchase online. You can find our crackers in the Deli or Specialty section of your local grocery store or check with your store manager.

Use Instacart to help you find Firehook in stock at a store near you or click here to order from Amazon Prime.

If you need any help locating our products near you, please reach out to us at info@firehook.com or call us toll free at 1-888-580-0745.

Can I buy Firehook online?

Yes! You can purchase on our Amazon store or on Instacart.

How do I keep my Firehook crackers fresh?

Our transparent package is resealable, ensuring that your crackers stay fresh even after you open the container. Please make sure to close the package tightly after each use.

Are your plastic packages recyclable?

Firehook’s plastic containers are 100% recyclable, making it easy for you to recycle and more efficient for municipalities to collect for recycling. The containers are made with 10% post-consumer recycled material and a grade of PET plastic with the lowest carbon footprint on the market today. Our labels are made from the only material currently available that is fully recyclable with a plastic pack.

Do you offer products for wholesale purchase?

Yes, learn more about our wholesale program at wholesale.firehook.com

PRODUCT QUESTIONS

Are Firehook crackers certified organic?

Our signature line of crackers is certified USDA Organic and Non-GMO Project verified. Certification on our limited-edition crackers may vary but adhere to strict ingredient sourcing guidelines.

Are Firehook crackers Kosher?

Our signature line of crackers is OK Kosher certified. Certification on our limited-edition crackers may vary but adhere to strict ingredient sourcing guidelines.

Are Firehook crackers gluten free?

Firehook crackers are not gluten free.

Are Firehook crackers seed oil free?

Yes, all varieties are made without added seed oils. Our signature line of crackers is made with organic olive oil and some select flavors are made with organic avocado oil.

Does Firehook use peanuts, tree nuts, soy, wheat or other allergens in your products?

All of our products are baked with wheat, and some flavors include sesame, milk or soy. If you have allergen-specific questions, please check our label, call us toll-free at 1-888-580-0745 or email us at info@firehook.com.

What is the shelf life of Firehook crackers?

Our signature line of crackers has a six-month shelf life. Please check the “best by” information on each package.
