Location is everything

In an AI-first world, accurate, real-time geolocation data is a critical input into fraud, operations, and personalization decisions.

But for most companies, the physical world is a blind spot. Location signals are missing, inaccurate, delayed, or easy to spoof, making them difficult to act on.

Radar is changing this, bringing physical world truth to every digital decision with the world’s most trusted location infrastructure and intelligence platform.

Today, thousands of companies, from high-growth startups to the Fortune 500, trust Radar for geolocation.
