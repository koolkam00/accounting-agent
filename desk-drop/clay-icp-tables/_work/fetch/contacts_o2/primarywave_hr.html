Shevonne James

Senior Human Resources Manager

“Jesus Loves Me”

“Jesus Loves Me” – Whitney Houston

Shevonne is the Head of Human Resources at Primary Wave serving as the Senior Human Resources Manager. As an experienced Human Resources professional with a career spanning more than 15 years in the field, she brings a well-versed background in recruitment, employee relations, benefits, training, talent acquisition, and more. Her career began in the healthcare industry, where she held roles at esteemed medical institutions such as Mount Sinai. She holds a Bachelor’s degree in Legal studies and a Master’s degree from Mercy University in Human Resources Management. After dedicating 13 years+ to healthcare, Shevonne made a strategic career pivot in 2021, transitioning into the entertainment industry. She joined Primary Wave Music as a Human Resources Manager—a role that perfectly aligned with her passions for music, law, and people. This transition not only marked a new chapter in her career but also highlighted the versatility of HR professionals across industries.

Sophia Popovits

NY Facilities and Culture Manager

“Living my life like it’s golden!”

“Golden” – Jill Scott

Sophia Popovits is the New York Facilities and Culture Manager at Primary Wave, where she has spent over seven years overseeing day-to-day operations, building systems, and organizing engaging experiences for the team. She began her career in entertainment in front of the camera before moving behind the scenes into hospitality and production across restaurants and live events. With more than a decade of experience, she’s no stranger to wearing multiple hats—something she brings to her role with a smile on her face. In addition to managing office operations, Sophia runs the internship program, growing it from a small initiative into a highly sought-after, job-ready pipeline for emerging music professionals. Outside of work, she continues to embrace her on-camera roots through her food content platform.
