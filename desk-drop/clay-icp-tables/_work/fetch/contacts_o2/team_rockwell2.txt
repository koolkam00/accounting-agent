About
Rockwell Group is a multidisciplinary architecture and design firm that balances creativity, innovation, and thought leadership with every undertaking.
Headquartered in New York City, with offices in Los Angeles and Madrid, the firm was founded in 1984 by David Rockwell, FAIA, and merges performance and architecture to create extraordinary experiences and built environments across the globe.
Today, David and the partners guide our 350-person firm to pursue the possibilities for connection and community. Our shared passion for theater, technology, craft, and people leads us to approach the big picture with as much importance as the smallest detail. We begin each of our projects by crafting a unique narrative that serves as a foundation, whether it be in hospitality, museum exhibitions, products, educational institutions, transportation hubs, or set design.
