We’re changing the status.
Our founders came from Wall Street. During their time there, Brandon Krieg and Ed Robinson both realized that wealth creation systems—particularly investing—were stacked against everyday Americans.
They left their jobs with a simple mission: make investing easy and affordable for everyone. Today, Stash is helping over 6 million people create a more secure financial future for themselves.

Ed Robinson
Co-CEO and Co-Founder
Ed is Co-CEO and Co-Founder of Stash, a company he founded with Brandon Krieg in 2015. For decades, Ed has focused on democratizing financial services through innovative products that are simple, affordable, and accessible. Stash's customer-centric platform, rooted in a personalized experience with advice and education, has helped customers set aside more than $3.5 billion on the platform to date—all via regular and automatic deposits. Early in his career, Ed provided financial advice to high-net-worth families focusing on asset allocation. Following that, he spent nearly a decade on equity trading desks across London and New York with Macquarie, where he met his eventual Co-Founder, Brandon Krieg.
