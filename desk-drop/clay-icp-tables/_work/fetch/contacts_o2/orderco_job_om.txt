Role Overview
As the Office Manager at Order.co’s new NYC headquarters in the heart of Flatiron, you’ll take ownership of all things related to our physical space, as well as some HR administrative support, to ensure the employee experience reflects our company values and culture. This role will report to our Director of People Operations, and is ideal for someone who is passionate about serving others, thrives on variety, and isn’t afraid to roll up their sleeves to get the job done - whether that means calling a plumber to fix a sink, shipping branded assets to new hires, or planning our annual holiday party.
What You'll Do
Own daily office operations, including supply management, kitchen stocking, mail handling, and package coordination
Coordinate facilities and vendors (cleaning, pest control, plant services, maintenance, construction projects, etc.)
Manage front-of-house experience including visitor logistics and reception support
Plan and execute office events, catered lunches, happy hours, team gatherings, and employee celebrations
Support executive hospitality for board meetings, investor visits, and leadership events
Oversee shared spaces including kitchens, meeting rooms, and common areas to ensure a clean and organized workplace
Manage office access, badges, and general security logistics
Handle employee operational requests and ad hoc office support
Support equipment stipend administration and operational tracking
Own office projects such as space improvements, branding, and workplace experience initiatives
Who You Are
3+ years of experience managing an office space, facilities, or similar roles
Organized and detail-oriented, with strong project management skills
Passionate about employee experience and creating a welcoming, inclusive, and efficient workspace
Gritty, hands-on, resilient, and unafraid to take on tasks big or small - from emptying the trash to organizing a company-wide event
Exceptional at context switching and managing multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment
A creative problem-solver who stays calm under pressure and finds innovative, cost-saving solutions
Comfortable working with external vendors, building management, and employees of all teams and levels at

Able to prioritize autonomously and eager to ask for help when needed

What you need to know about the NYC Tech Scene
As the undisputed financial capital of the world, New York City is an epicenter of startup funding activity. The city has a thriving fintech scene and is a major player in verticals ranging from AI to biotech, cybersecurity and digital media. It also has universities like NYU, Columbia and Cornell Tech attracting students and researchers from across the globe, providing the ecosystem with a constant influx of world-class talent. And its East Coast location and three international airports make it a perfect spot for European companies establishing a foothold in the United States.
