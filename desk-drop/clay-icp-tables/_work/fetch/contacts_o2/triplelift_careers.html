Core values

Working at TripleLift

TripleLift’s greatest asset is our people. We’ve purposely nurtured a passionate, innovative, and collaborative environment—and our people show up every day to make a difference.
Benefits

Your best life…backed by TripleLift

Together, we make it happen. At TripleLift, we believe that to be at your best, you need balance, quality healthcare, and a positive working environment.

Health

We offer competitive Medical, Dental, and Vision individual plans and company paid short-term and long-term disability insurance (benefits vary by country).

Wellness

We care about your wellbeing. You can meditate with a Headspace subscription or receive personalized mental wellbeing, physical care, and financial advice through third-party company-sponsored partnerships. We also offer paid parental leave to primary and non-primary caregivers.

Nourishing Work Culture

Connection is a core part of the TripleLift culture. In addition to our employee resource groups, we cultivate a strong sense of community by offering a variety of fun, engaging, and growth-oriented opportunities. These are provided monthly through our virtual Lift Lab sessions, events, and company-wide Pulse meetings, ensuring that everyone stays connected and informed.

Learning & Development

We encourage learning and development at all career stages. TripleLift is rooted in its core value, “Learn & Grow’ through offering a wealth of programs to employees. All TripleLifters have access to an annual learning stipend, coaching and mentoring opportunities, internal rotation programs and more. We believe with continuous learning, we grow together as a team and as individuals.

Tenure Celebrations

We celebrate your milestones! At your 5-year anniversary, you’ll enjoy a 1-month sabbatical to take a well-deserved break and do something just for you.

Work-Life Balance

We understand the need for a healthy work-life balance. We provide paid time off and leave options to help you recharge, reset, or take care of personal needs when it counts.

Important message about our interviewing and hiring practices

If you believe that you have become victim to an active scam and experienced financial loss, please report the matter to law enforcement and your financial institutions.

We have become aware of individuals being contacted by scammers who are impersonating TripleLift employees and offering fake employment opportunities. This includes a specific entity operating out of Zambia, calling itself “TL Intern,” which is in no way affiliated with TripleLift. All legitimate public job postings can be confirmed on our official careers page.

While our recruiters may sometimes reach out about non-public jobs, all official communication from TripleLift employees will come from
a @triplelift.com email address. Please remember that our recruiters will never ask for banking information, issue checks, or request
compensation from a candidate.

If you believe you have become a victim of an active scam and have experienced financial loss, we urge you to report the matter to law
enforcement and your financial institutions.
