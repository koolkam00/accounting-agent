Our values
We are a collaborative, multi-disciplinary team working to change how the real estate industry gets things done. Our values shape how we work together, approach decisions, and make a difference.
Bold and ambitious
Orbital aims high, challenges the status quo, and solves problems without an obvious solution.
Power to our people
Our teams are trusted to take ownership, use their own judgment, and find the best way forward.
Question or commit
We encourage open debate and respectfully challenging ideas, allowing us to collaboratively identify the best approach.
Eat that frog
