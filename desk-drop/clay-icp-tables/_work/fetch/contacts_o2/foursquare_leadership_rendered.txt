The industry-leading platform for all things geospatial

From beloved consumer app to industry leader — that’s our journey in a nutshell. We pioneered the whole ‘check-in’ thing, but we’ve come a long way since then. We realized the power of our technology to other businesses, so we decided to think bigger.

Today, we’ve pivoted to become the #1 geospatial platform, offering solutions for developers, marketers, data scientists and more. With proprietary technology, continuous innovation, and a strong focus on consumer privacy, we’re helping businesses across various industries drive real world outcomes.
