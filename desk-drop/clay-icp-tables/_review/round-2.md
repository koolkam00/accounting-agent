Reviewer: independent adversarial reviewer, not the builder. Date 2026-10-24. Paths are relative to `desk-drop/`. I wrote only to `clay-icp-tables/_review/` (this file and `_review/r2/`). Tool scripts and the LibreOffice profile lived in my scratchpad. I sent nothing, signed up for nothing, bought nothing and made no git writes. I did not open LinkedIn or sec.gov. Every page I cite was opened with a curl GET that carried a generic browser User-Agent and no personal identifier. The pages are saved gzipped under `_review/r2/fetch/`. Geocoding went through `_shared/geocode.py`, one call at a time. ## Verdict

The round-1 fixes are real. The health-claim gate now reads product pages, and all eight named brands fail. The Friday sync no longer pastes over the tables. The daily views are INDEX/MATCH and compute in LibreOffice 24.2. `dnc_any` blocks a whole company group. `score_reason` and `channel` follow the founder's controls. I re-ran the daily-view rule in an edited copy, and it matches my own implementation of CONTRACTS 6.2. Columns match 6.2 exactly. No personal email, phone or guessed address appears anywhere.

I would still not sign it as it stands:

1. **Blocker.** Brand `hq_city` cells store street addresses copied from privacy policies. NYC's tax-lot data shows at least two of them are homes, a three-family walk-up apartment and a two-family house. One of these rows (B-SIDES) is in today's daily view. CONTRACTS section 2 forbids home addresses.
2. **Estimate method.** For a company headquartered outside New York, the Tuesday estimate turns leased square feet into a floor of full seats, with no in-office rate. The build sheet does not describe this rule. It lifts daily-view #1 (Alpine) and #3 (Signal AI), and 12 more top-50 rows, to full onsite points. Meanwhile NYC-headquartered firms with large new leases are gated out on stale counts.
3. **Several controls are leakier than the docs say:**
   - implied-effect wording on three ready brands has no blocking review;
   - the weekly store check never reaches E's draft maker;
   - 24 brand reasons misstate the contact points;
   - a few contacts do not trace to a source;
   - two company LinkedIn links point at the wrong company.

Counts: **1 blocker, 7 majors, 9 minors.**

Sample error rates this round: offices **2.3%** (86 fields checked, 2 wrong) and brands **7.7%** (78 fields, 6 wrong). One brand field (Goodie Girl's "CEO") has no opened source and no unverified note. That meets the spot-check log's own definition of fabricated, so this round does not count toward the brief's "two consecutive clean rounds" stop rule on the brand side.
