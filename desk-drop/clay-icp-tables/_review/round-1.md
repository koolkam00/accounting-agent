# Workstream A (Clay ICP tables): independent review, round 1

Reviewer: independent adversarial reviewer. I did not build this. Date 2026-10-24. Paths are relative to `desk-drop/`. I wrote only to `clay-icp-tables/_review/`, sent nothing, signed up for nothing, and did not open LinkedIn or sec.gov. Every page I cite was opened with a curl GET that sent a generic User-Agent and no personal identifier. The opened pages are saved in `clay-icp-tables/_review/fetch/`.

## Verdict

The build is careful in most places. It keeps sources and dates, keeps names out unless a page shows them, records no personal data and no guessed emails, matches the CONTRACTS 6.2 columns exactly, and has a daily view that computes correctly in a SORT/FILTER-capable engine. I would not sign it yet, for two reasons:

1. **The brand health-claim gate was only checked against homepages.** Eight of the top 60 brands make explicit structure/function or disease claims on their own product pages and still pass the gate. Two of them are in today's daily view.
2. **The build sheet's Friday sync step breaks the do-not-contact rule.** Pasting the Clay export over the Sheet tabs overwrites the view formulas, and an opted-out row comes back at rank 1 of the daily view.

Below those two, the office side is thin on outreach-ready supply. There are 26 ready rows, which last about a week. Office LinkedIn URLs and backup contacts are missing, the Tuesday estimates are inflated where the row's own leased square footage says otherwise, and the morning routine takes about 22 to 25 minutes, not 15.

Counts: **2 blockers, 8 majors, 9 minors.**

### Routine walk (today's views)

Step 4 allows 9 minutes for ten rows, the top five of each view.

- **Offices.** All 5 rows need "find the person on LinkedIn and paste the URL" (0 of 50 top offices have a URL). Two of the five also say "adjacent title: ask who runs the pantry". The steps are: LinkedIn search, open the profile, confirm title and employer, copy, switch to the Offices tab, find the `row_id`, paste. That takes about 60 to 90 seconds a row.
- **Brands.** 3 of 5 need the LinkedIn lookup. All 5 need a Whole Foods re-check: the given link is a site search that needs the store set by hand, and today's top five use two different stores. Three need a founder-review note read. That is about 2 minutes a row.

Step 4 alone comes to about 15 to 17 minutes. With logging, export and queueing, the whole routine is about 22 to 25 minutes. A day with replies takes longer, because each reply touches 4 to 6 columns (`status`, `last_reply_date`, `reply_type`, `next_action`, `next_action_date`, and on an opt-out `do_not_contact` and `opt_out_date`). A `wrong_person` swap means copying 13 backup columns into the primary columns, spread across the sheet.
