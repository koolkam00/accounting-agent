## The boundary in one paragraph

The boundary runs from 14th St to 30th St and from Sixth Ave to Park Ave S. Both frontages of each edge street and avenue are inside. It also takes in the Union Square frame: University Place to Third Ave, between 13th and 15th St. That holds Whole Foods Union Square, 841-853 Broadway, Zero Irving, Trader Joe's 14th St and the Target block. The area is about 1 km², 1.4 km north to south and 0.74 km across. It holds 270 of the 276 sourced company rows and 11 of the 30 stores. It follows the Wikipedia definitions of the Flatiron District and NoMad. The reasoning, and what was left out and why, are on the Method tab.

## One-minute phone test (do this before the walk)

Untested on a real phone so far: unverified, founder to confirm. Some phone previews (iOS Mail, the Files preview, some cloud-drive previews) show HTML without running its JavaScript.

1. Send `map-private.html` to yourself privately and open it on the phone.
2. If you see the map with coloured dots, tap **Find**, type "853", tap the building, and check the popup list scrolls. Done.

## Make a map for one brand

Needs a laptop with Python 3 (with pandas) and Node 22 with Playwright (`npm install -g playwright`, then `npx playwright install chromium`). This is a step for whoever has that laptop, not a phone step.

0. Once: make a small Python environment with fontTools, from this folder: `python3 -m venv v && v/bin/pip install fonttools`. Below, `v/bin/python` is that Python.
1. Find the brand's stores in `retailers.csv` (use the brand's own store locator or the retailer's store selector; never guess). Note their `retailer_id`s in the order the proposal's store table lists them.
2. If a store is missing, add it to `_work/retailers_research.csv` with a source and date, then run `_build/run_all.sh` once.
3. From this folder run `python3 _build/public_map.py v/bin/python --brand <brand-slug> --stores <id1>,<id2>`, then `NODE_PATH="$(npm root -g)" node _build/render.js .`. The map lands in `brand-maps/<brand-slug>.png` and `.svg`. Pin numbers follow the order of `--stores`. Add `--labels numbers` for numbered pins only, when the proposal's store table carries the names (the default, `--labels names`, prints the names on the map). If a step in 2 needs a rebuild, use `_build/run_all.sh v/bin/python`.
4. In D's proposal, set the map picture to that PNG.

## What is not done (also in REPORT.md)

- **A privacy rule was broken during research.** On 2026-10-23 or 24 (the exact time was not logged), research slice S1 sent **the account owner's email address in the User-Agent header** of a request to **www.sec.gov**. It was downloading the 215 Park Ave S loan prospectus, and SEC EDGAR refuses requests without a contact email (`_work/notes_S1.md`, line 51).
  - Slice S2 also used EDGAR and data.sec.gov, and did not record what it sent, so it may have done the same. Its session has ended, so this cannot be confirmed.
  - The assembler opened one EDGAR page with no personal identifier.
  - No file here stores the owner's identifiers.
  - The 12 rows that cite sec.gov say so in their notes (B-C-033 to 039, B-C-047, B-C-192, 196, 197 and 203). You may open those links in a browser.
  - No build script calls sec.gov, and no agent will open it again.
  - This is the full account. REPORT.md repeats it under "Rule breaches".
- No lobby is verified on foot. 64 of 81 buildings have an unknown lobby type, and every building's certificate-of-insurance rule is unknown.
- Headcounts are the weakest field. Review round 2 tested every sourced band against a second public count (Revelio Labs pages, as of March 2026, or the company's own page): 21 agree, 22 disagree and are now `unknown` with both counts in the row, and 10 firms turned out to be over 500 and moved to Near-miss. 45 of the 162 candidates still carry a band (44 in the four bands plus Normal Computing's under-40 founder's call; 21 confirmed by a second count, 23 single-source); 26 of those are occupied. 117 have an unknown band, and each row says why.
- Bands are whole-company unless a source counts the New York office (2 rows). 12 banded candidates are based outside New York (`hq_in_nyc = N`) and 3 have an unknown HQ (Foursquare's sources conflict), so their New York office may be much smaller than the band.
- Review round 3: Built In profiles (employer-editable) and Deskpass or LiquidSpace listings (operator-written) no longer count as the independent confirmation of a company's or operator's own page. TripleLift, BrainPOP and Seedtag are now medium, as are eight coworking sites; a walk-by or a press source can raise them. Six candidates placed only by an undated profile (Bombas, Morning Brew, Daily Harvest, Retool, Wellcom, accessiBe) are now `occupancy-unconfirmed`.
- 17 rows whose only current source was an undated Built In, Deskpass or LiquidSpace profile are now `low` (7 candidates, 2 exclusions, 8 near-miss). Nothing dated places them at the address; the walk or the company's own page can raise them.
- Two planned moves (BrightTower, Corcoran) have passed without a source confirming them: both are `occupancy-unconfirmed` until the lobby directory shows them.
- LinkedIn company URLs are blank: LinkedIn was not opened.
- No store row claims a store carries a named brand. Brand-to-store evidence belongs in the Clay brand table.
