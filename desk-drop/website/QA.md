# QA: pass bar, tests and results

The pass bar below was written on 2026-10-24 **before the first line of the site was built**. It does not move to fit results. Changes made in review are marked where they are made: they make a check stricter (P9, P12, P14, P15, P19, P20), correct a mistake in the bar itself (P11), or add a check the bar missed (P21, review round 3). Results are filled in by round at the end of this file; the commands that produce them are in `tests/README.md`.
