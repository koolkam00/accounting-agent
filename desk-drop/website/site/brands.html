What we never promise

Sales, lift or new customers. We commit to the work: placement within one business day, the photos, the survey, the report and the debrief. Results are reported as we observe them.

What the report contains

One page, as a PDF, within seven business days of the drop.

  1. Header: your brand and product, the cluster, the drop date and the window.
  2. Roster: each office by industry and headcount band, with units placed, units gone by day 5, and survey responses.
  3. Evidence: two photos per office, at placement and on day 3 or 5.
  4. Survey: the number of responses, how many tried it, the average rating, how many would buy, and three comments word for word.
  5. Engagement: QR scans by office, and code redemptions to date.
  6. What we learned: three points, including anything to change, such as flavor, size or card copy.
  7. Recommendation: renew into which offices, adjust what, or stop.
  8. Limitations: the same plain paragraph, every time.

What we count, and what we don't

This is why you can trust the report. Each rung is counted on its own, with how we know it and what it cannot tell you. A scan is never reported as a sale, and we do not claim lift.

  1. Delivered

    Counted at handoff, at each office.

    Limit: none.

  2. Placed

    The placement photo, card in view, with a count of the units you can see.

    Limit: the pantry owner may hold some back for later.

  3. Depleted

    Counts from photos or from the pantry owner on day 3 and day 5.

    Limit: one person may take several; sharing and disposal are invisible.

  4. Responded

    Answers to the anonymous three-question survey. "Tried it" is one of the answers.

    Limit: self-selected, small samples.

  5. Scanned

    One QR code per office, so scans are counted office by office.

    Limit: a scan is curiosity, not intent.

  6. Purchased

    Redemptions of your campaign code, which you report to us at day 30.

    Limit: some would have bought anyway.

  7. Incremental

    Not measured. We say so in writing, in every report.

Two more numbers appear when they exist. Exposure: each office's Tuesday headcount, as a range, because not everyone visits the pantry. Retailer sales: if your distributor or data provider can pull store-level sales for the nearest store two weeks before and after, we add them, marked as suggestive only.

How a campaign runs

  1. Before anything shipsEach office on the route sees your brand first and can say no. You sign, pay, and approve the card text.
  2. 10 business days before the dropYou ship the units plus the 10% reserve, freight prepaid, to one Manhattan address.
  3. 5 business days beforeWe count every case and check best-by dates, lot numbers and damage. Any shortfall goes to you the same day.
  4. Drop day, Tuesday or Wednesday morningOne labeled case per office, on the shelf within one business day, photographed with the card in view.
  5. Day 3A second photo. We top up from your reserve where the shelf has run out.
  6. Day 5The window and the survey close. Leftovers come back to you at your cost, or stay with the office, as you chose when you signed.
  7. Within 7 business days of the dropYour one-page report, then a 20-minute debrief.

If your product is late: if it has not arrived three business days before the drop, the drop moves one week, and every office hears the same day.

Straight answers

Can we run a free trial first?
No. We don't run free pilots. The founding price is the trial, and it stays locked for your next two campaigns.
Do you guarantee sales?
No. We commit to the work, and we report what we observe. Nothing in the report is counted as a sale unless it is a redemption of your code, and even then some of those people would have bought anyway.
Can we have the employees' emails?
No. We collect no personal data about employees, and the survey is anonymous. If you choose, the survey can offer your newsletter: anyone who ticks an unticked box has their email passed to you, under your own privacy policy.
Which offices?
Offices with about 40 to 250 people in on a Tuesday, each with a pantry owner who can say yes alone. The proposal lists them by neighborhood, industry and Tuesday headcount. You get the office names once you sign.
What if an office does not place it?
An office that has not placed your product by the day after the drop comes off your invoice, or its share is refunded ($150, or $120 at the founding price), unless we can replace it with another office on the roster.
Can we cancel?
Yes, by email, until the drop day. Before the cancel-by date, 10 business days before the drop: a full refund less $100. After that, we keep 50%. From the drop day on, the full price is due. If your product is late, the drop moves one week at no charge.
Who else is in the drop?
One to three brands per drop, and never two in the same category.
How is the product handled?
Sealed cases only, kept at room temperature, clean and dry. We never open, repack or relabel anything.

The full terms are in a short campaign agreement, which you see before you pay.
